SMS Consent Disclosure

McCartney Automation provides automated text messaging services on behalf of client businesses (e.g., HVAC and plumbing companies). This page describes exactly how customers of these businesses consent to receive text messages.

How Consent Is Collected

When a customer calls a client business's published phone number and the call is not answered, the caller hears the following recorded greeting before leaving a message:

"Thank you for calling [Business Name]. We're unable to take your call right now — please leave a message, and we'll text you back to schedule a callback. By leaving a message, you agree to receive a text message from us regarding your call. Message and data rates may apply."

By continuing to leave a message after hearing this disclosure, the caller consents to receive a text message from the business regarding their call, including a missed-call follow-up, a callback confirmation, and — if they go on to complete a service with the business — a one-time request for a Google review.

No Other Opt-In Method

This recorded verbal disclosure is the sole opt-in method used. There is no web form, keyword, or other opt-in path for this campaign.

Message Frequency

Message frequency varies based on the customer's interactions with the business (e.g., one message per missed call, one message per completed service).

Message and Data Rates

Message and data rates may apply.

Opt-Out

Reply STOP at any time to stop receiving messages. Reply HELP for assistance.
