Privacy Policy — McCartney Automation

McCartney Automation ("we," "us") provides automated text messaging services on behalf of client businesses (e.g., HVAC and plumbing companies). This policy explains how we handle your information when you interact with these services.

Information We Collect

Your mobile phone number, obtained when you call a client business's phone line or complete a service with them.

How We Use It

To send you automated text messages, including missed-call follow-ups and service review requests, on behalf of the business you contacted.

No Sharing of Mobile Numbers

We do not sell, rent, or share your mobile phone number with third parties for marketing purposes. Your number is used solely to deliver the messages described above.

Message Frequency

Message frequency varies based on your interactions with the business (e.g., one message per missed call, one message per completed service).
